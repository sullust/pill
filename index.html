Add it to your iPhone home screen

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, turn it off so the app opens in Safari, where you're already signed in to Claude.
  4. Tap Add.

The home-screen icon opens Pills Left directly.
